# Refund policy (v2, effective 1 July 2026, supersedes v1)

Scope: all paid plans (pro, enterprise). Free-tier accounts have nothing to refund.

1. A customer may request a full refund within 30 days of a charge, for any reason.
   (v1 allowed 14 days; the window was extended on 1 July 2026.)
2. Between 31 and 60 days after a charge, a refund is granted only when the request
   cites a documented service outage affecting the customer's account.
3. After 60 days no refund is issued; offer account credit instead.
4. Enterprise customers under an annual contract follow the contract, not this policy.
5. Every refund decision must be recorded with the policy version applied.
